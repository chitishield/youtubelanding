Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 4, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how YT Bookmarks ("we", "us", "our") collects, uses, and protects information when you use our browser extension and website (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account information: your email address when you create an account.
  • Bookmark data: video timestamps, notes, and tags you save. Free-tier data is stored locally in your browser; synced data on paid plans is stored on our servers.
  • Payment information: handled by our payment provider — we never see or store your full card details.
  • Usage data: basic analytics such as feature usage and error logs, used to improve the Service.

2. How We Use Your Information

  • To provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • To process payments and manage your subscription or license
  • To communicate with you about your account, updates, or support requests
  • To detect, prevent, and address technical issues or misuse

3. Data Storage & Security

Free-tier bookmarks are stored locally in your browser and never transmitted to our servers. Synced data on paid plans is encrypted in transit and stored with reputable third-party infrastructure providers. We take reasonable measures to protect your data, but no method of transmission or storage is 100% secure.

4. Third-Party Services

We use third-party services for payment processing, hosting, and analytics. YT Bookmarks is not affiliated with YouTube or Google; any interaction with YouTube's own services is governed by YouTube's own privacy policy.

5. Data Retention

We retain account data for as long as your account is active or as needed to comply with legal obligations. You may request deletion of your account and associated data at any time.

6. Your Rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

7. Children's Privacy

The Service is not directed to individuals under 16, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Last updated" date above.

9. Contact Us

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be sent to support@ytbookmarks.com.
